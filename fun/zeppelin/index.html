They arrive as infrastructure

Listening now is strange because they do not arrive as merely an old band. They arrive as infrastructure. You recognize the guitar shape before you consciously remember hearing it. Fifty years of other bands have borrowed the thunder, the pauses, the silhouette, and the idea that a riff can be architecture. Discovering the source after all those descendants is like meeting somebody whose face you already know from an entire bloodline.
