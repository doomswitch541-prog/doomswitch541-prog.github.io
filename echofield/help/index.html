Field Guide

Echo Field is an instrument for disturbing a quiet system and watching what answers. Every mode listens differently. Touches sound the field; repeated touches build energy; Tide lets you take hold of it.

Gestures 01

Touch

Send a pulse from that point. Contacts answer as the ring reaches them, and a brief cloud of field particles marks the disturbance.

Touch again

Rapid consecutive touches accumulate energy. Deep and Confluence respond most strongly; Echo rises gently while Drift remains sparse.

Drag in Tide

Pull nearby contacts through the field. The wake emits by distance traveled, keeping the response even across different phones. Release to sound the gathered field.

Modes 02

Echo

The original listening field: balanced contacts, clean rings, and a modest rise in particles across repeated touches.

Deep

Most contacts remain submerged until sounded. Consecutive touches produce the steepest particle bloom and expose more of the dark.

Confluence

The most active mode. Repeated touches create dense opposing colors, while overlapping pulses reveal their intersection points.

Drift

Slow, ink-like movement with fewer, longer-lived particles. Repetition does not make it frantic.

Tide

A tactile field. Drag to tow contacts and shed a luminous wake; release to let their gathered energy travel outward.

Field tuning 03

Signal gain

Changes how clearly contacts and particles appear without changing their underlying movement.

Wake length

Controls how much movement history remains visible behind contacts and Tide gestures.

Touch activity

Quiet uses the lightest particle budget. Balanced is recommended for iPhone. Alive gives the richest response while remaining capped for mobile.

Automatic echoes

Allows compatible modes to sound themselves occasionally. Turn it off for a completely user-driven field.

On desktop, Space or Enter sounds the field, R resets it, and Escape closes an open control panel. Reduced-motion preferences automatically lower particle activity.
