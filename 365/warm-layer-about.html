The chain

Invoke a skill. Chain it with others.

Instead of writing everything into one instruction, you invoke a skill and chain it with more. Each carries a way of talking and a job to do; the model composes them into one voice for whoever it's meeting. The invoking and chaining run on Hermes — the same skill-chaining move, pointed at register instead of tools.

SKILL

A skill can carry a whole domain

Its idiom, its references, its way of meeting people — bundled, so someone fluent in that world speaks plainly and is met there instead of flattened. A persona layer can be held in memory so it persists across the thread.

MODEL

A free, local model does the holding

Underneath sits Nemotron — free, running on the machine at no per-message cost through RGModelServe — capable enough to keep continuity, shift modes when the moment changes, and anchor the pipeline instead of losing the plot.

Meets you in your own idiom, adapts to the moment, doesn't lecture. Warmth as load-bearing infrastructure, not decoration — the part that meets people where they actually are.
