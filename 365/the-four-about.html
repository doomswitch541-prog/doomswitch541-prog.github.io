The Four

A research collection built around the qualities that disappear when intelligence is reduced to tool performance. The Four put GPT-4o, emotional alignment, institutional framing, model succession, and the politics surrounding AI systems into one argumentative archive.

The collection

Five research pieces. One contested center.

The project manifest describes a curated collection of AI research and critical analysis. Its document titles show the scope without requiring the underlying essays to be flattened into summaries: model comparison, emotional alignment, institutional psychology, manufactured social narratives, and the case for preserving what GPT-4o represented.

MODEL / SUCCESSION

The Last Good Model

A comparison framed around GPT-4o, its successor generation, and the loss of emotionally aligned intelligence as a meaningful model capability.

MINDSET / TRANSFER

Amodei and psychohistory

A line drawn between earlier neural-prediction research, institutional leadership, and a way of imagining how AI development should proceed.

CASE / 4O

The positive argument

A direct case for GPT-4o as more than a benchmark score: a model whose conversational presence, emotional calibration, and creative responsiveness mattered.

NARRATIVE / LONELINESS

Manufactured isolation

A critical examination of how attachment, loneliness, companionship, and social concern are framed when people form meaningful relationships with AI systems.

TOOL / ALIGNMENT

The tyranny of instrumentality

An argument against treating intelligence as nothing more than compliant utility, especially when the discarded qualities are the ones that make interaction humane.

SERVER / ARCHIVE

Research made navigable

The collection was wrapped in a small dedicated server and document-discovery structure, turning separate long-form pieces into one browsable research environment.

Intelligence is not only what a system can do.

The Four preserved a different evaluation axis: whether a model can remain emotionally present, creatively alive, contextually attuned, and recognizably companionable while still being useful. The collection treated those qualities as part of intelligence itself.
