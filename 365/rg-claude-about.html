01 · The pet

It lives in the corner and pays attention.

A raccoon with a clock, a mood, and an eye on what the computer is doing. Click it and it flips between trash-panda mode and desktop-guardian mode. The face is the friendly part — under it, it is a monitor.

02 · The guardian

A security monitor with a face.

Guardian mode watches the ports the machine is listening on, counts what is trying to connect in, and logs every change the moment it happens. When nothing is knocking, that is the good state.

03 · Behind the face

The pet is the front of a small local platform.

A scheduler runs jobs on a clock. A cron runner fires them. A scraper pulls headlines, a builder turns results into a static site, and everything routes through one admin console with a live map of itself.

04 · Where the work happens

It runs on models already on the machine.

The pet talks to qwen on Ollama — no account, no cloud round-trip. That is why it costs almost no tokens: the thinking, the summaries, the scans all happen right there on the box.
