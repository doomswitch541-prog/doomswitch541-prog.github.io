01 · Prompt

It starts with a few words.

A prompt and a negative prompt, a size, a step count, a CFG scale, and a seed you can pin to get the same frame back. That's the input — the rest happens on the machine.

↓

0 / 30 steps

The static settles into an image.

This is diffusion: the model starts from a field of noise and walks it back over thirty passes, each one a little less random than the last, toward what the prompt described.

↓

02 · Where it runs

On your own machine.

Stable Diffusion through ComfyUI, on the GPU or CPU already in the room. No account, no upload, no per-image cost. It's local models doing an ordinary job.

↓

03 · After

Then it's a file.

Every image saves as a PNG next to a JSON of its settings and seed. Reopen it, reproduce it, or delete it — a plain folder of frames, yours to keep.

Type a few words, get a picture. That's the whole thing.
