What is Luxury Tier Homelessness?

Luxury tier homelessness refers to the phenomenon where individuals with high incomes cannot access basic housing and services that are available to those with even slightly higher incomes. This creates a paradoxical situation where homelessness exists at the highest levels of society.

Despite economic prosperity, the structure of housing, job markets, and services creates a tiered system where access to basic needs becomes dependent on income levels beyond mere survival.

Addressing the Luxury Tier Paradox

  • Implement luxury tax on high-end services and amenities
  • Expand affordable housing near luxury districts
  • Subsidize basic services for high-income but housing-insecure individuals
  • Create tiered pricing models for essential services
  • Implement inclusion policies in luxury housing developments
