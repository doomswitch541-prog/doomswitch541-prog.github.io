The journey

Every horizon becomes somewhere you can go.

The world opens outward without losing its center. Raccoon Village remains home, but roads lead into orchard trouble, crowded shallows, mountain crossings, buried infrastructure, communications gardens, and the long approach to Delta City.

EXPLORE

Twenty-two connected regions

Fields, wetlands, caves, passes, gardens, transit chambers, and cities form one continuous journey. Each new region changes the atmosphere without feeling detached from the world around it.

TRAVERSE

Paths exist under your feet

Movement follows real roads, ramps, stairs, bridges, and layered walkways. Delta can rise from the bay through lower streets, elevated gardens, and summit architecture without turning the climb into a menu choice.

MEET

People remember why they are there

Characters carry continuing conversations, local concerns, and story threads that meet the place around them. A garden listens; a scholar waits; trouble gathers in orchards, paddies, and shallows.

ATTUNE

A loadout that belongs to each weapon

Weapons hold their own sphere arrangements, granted arts, linked paths, and enhancements. Changing equipment changes the shape of a build without making every weapon feel interchangeable.

The world keeps listening.

Raccoon Gang grows by making the next place worth reaching. A distant structure becomes a walkable city. A passage becomes a remembered route. A person becomes part of an ongoing story. The result is a world that feels larger because its details remain connected.
