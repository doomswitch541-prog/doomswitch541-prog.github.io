Inside the Board

Every caller, destination response, and shift closing is written into this page. Nothing is generated, fetched, or assembled from phrase pieces. The board makes no network requests.

Four complete five-call decks rotate over eight nights. Each deck stays on duty for two local calendar nights. Exchange calls use about 9–26 seconds on Normal or 3–9 seconds on Quick. Clerk mode tightens that to about 3–7 seconds and fills the gap with a spoken hold line when Voice is on. The first call arrives sooner.

Optional Voice uses only speech voices the browser marks as installed on this device. Voice Reel lets you choose among those local voices. It needs no microphone, account, key, or speech-service request. When no local voice is available, the board stays text-only.

Cut Voice stops a queued or speaking line immediately; Replay Line starts the last complete caller or operator note again. Calls narration reads callers and connections; Full shift also reads the board briefing, complete closing, and five-line log. Kwik-E-Mart mode makes the selected device voice faster, higher, and musically over-courteous. While the next caller approaches, the clerk keeps the counter talking instead of leaving dead air.

Settings and this five-call shift stay in local browser storage when it is available. Leaving the tab pauses an unanswered timer; returning schedules a fresh interval.
